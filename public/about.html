Klaxon

An online quizbowl buzzing system.

Klaxon is a buzzer for online quizbowl.

It's built to be fair across locations and connection speeds. Your device records the moment you press and sends it with the buzz. The server tracks each player's latency and waits briefly after the first buzz to decide who was first. Safeguards stop players from gaming this.

Moderators read, score and see buzzes on one screen with built-in MODAQ. Players see the scoresheet. A second staff member can join as scorekeeper.

Players can alert the moderator when the buzzer needs a reset, and everyone sees when a player disconnects.

Tournament tools handle join links and coordination between moderators and directors. An account keeps your preferences across tournaments.
