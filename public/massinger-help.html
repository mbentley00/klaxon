MASSINGER tournaments

Before each game, the two teams take turns banning and protecting subcategories of the packet's tossups. The first team bans. Then the second team bans and protects, then the first team bans and protects, and so on until each team has made 3 bans. The last ban ends it.

A 26-tossup packet becomes a 20-tossup game. The banned tossups become the tiebreakers, in packet order: if the game is tied after 20, question 21 is the first tossup that was banned.

For the director

  1. Create a team tournament with MASSINGER on.
  2. Upload packets that qualify.
  3. Upload the roster.
  4. Add rooms and line up moderators.
  5. Release each round on the day.

1. Create the tournament

On Create a tournament, tick MASSINGER pick/ban before each game (at the bottom of the scoring format). Then choose:

  • Who makes the picks. Anyone on the team (the default), Each team's captain, Moderator only (players call their picks out loud), or Nobody: random bans, no protects, where Klaxon bans 6 tossups at random and the teams don't pick.
  • The timer, in seconds per pick (0 for none). When it runs out, Klaxon makes a random pick of the kind that's due.

You can change both later in the tournament's settings, and a moderator can change them for one game.

2. Upload packets that qualify

Each round's packet needs:

  • More than 20 tossups. 26 uses all six bans.
  • A subcategory on every tossup, as the last part of its metadata, after the last comma: <Author, Science - Biology>.
  • At least two different subcategories.

A packet that doesn't qualify is read in full, without a pick/ban. Upload packets on the Packets & roster tab. New rounds start hidden from moderators.

3. Upload the roster

Also on Packets & roster. Moderators then choose teams from a list, and with roster joining on, players pick their team and name when they join.

4. Rooms and moderators

Add rooms on the Rooms tab. Send each moderator their room's reader link, or send everyone the moderator invite link from Moderators & links and approve their requests. Share the player landing page or the room links with teams.

5. On the day

Click Release next packet for each round. Moderators can only load rounds you've released.

For moderators

  1. Pick the round, Start reading.
  2. Set the teams, Begin pick/ban.
  3. Players pick. Continue to the game.
  4. Read, then Save & next game.

1. Pick the round

Open your reader link, or Read here on the invite page. Click the released round, then Start reading.

2. Set up the game

Choose the two teams. Every connected player has to be on one of them, because the players make the picks. Untick anyone who starts on the bench. Under Pick/ban, check who picks first (a coin flip unless you choose), who makes the picks, and the seconds per pick. Then click Begin pick/ban.

3. The pick/ban

The players pick on their own screens, and you watch it happen. The screen shows whose turn it is, the clock, and the last pick. You can also make a pick yourself (the Ban and Protect buttons), use Random ban, Undo last or Edit picks, or Restart.

When it's done, click Continue to the game.

With random bans, there's one button, Draw the bans, and Redraw if you need to start over.

4. Read

The game has the 20 questions that are left. Buzzes appear in the panel on the right: the player's name and team pop up in large type, and the window's edges flash, so you won't miss one while you're reading. At the end, click Save & next game. This files the game and downloads a backup copy, then goes back to the setup screen for the next round.

For players

Join the room and pick your team and name. While the moderator sets up the game, the pick/ban panel says Starting soon.

When it's your team's turn, your screen says so in large type, and each subcategory has a Ban or Protect button. Anyone on the team can pick unless the director chose captains or the moderator. When it's the other team's turn, you watch the picks arrive.

The pick/ban sounds have their own switch, Pick sounds on/off, at the top of the panel. It doesn't affect the buzz sound.

After the games

Stats work as in any tournament. Every exported game records its picks and bans by category, and the buzzpoints download includes a klaxon_massinger_picks.csv. Buzzes are numbered by the full packet, so each buzz lands on the right question even though each room read a different 20. If a packet was edited after a game was played on it, klaxon_numbering_warnings.json in the zip lists the games to check.

Questions

A player clicked too late and their pick didn't go through.

Picks are refused if the board has moved on since that screen last updated. The player sees why and can pick again. After a team's ban, there's a 2.5-second pause before the same team's protect, so nobody protects by accident with a double click.

Someone made the wrong pick.

The moderator can use Undo last, or Edit picks to change any pick.

A player can't pick: it says the room doesn't know their team.

On the moderator's Set up the game screen, put that player on their team.

The game is tied after 20 questions.

Read on: question 21 is the first banned tossup in packet order, then the next banned one, and so on.

One packet has no subcategories.

That round is read in full, with no pick/ban. The others are unaffected.
